# Customer Onboarding OKR

Objective: Help new customers reach first value faster and with less support.

KR1: Increase the share of new accounts that complete the first-value workflow within 7 days from 42% to 60% by the end of Q3.

KR2: Reduce median time from signup to first successful workflow from 3.8 days to under 2 days by the end of Q3.

KR3: Reduce onboarding-related support tickets per new account from 0.42 to 0.30 by the end of Q3.

Grade: A-. The Objective names a clear customer outcome, and each Key Result has a baseline, target, and deadline. The main constraint is keeping the first-value workflow and onboarding-ticket taxonomy stable through Q3.
