# [DRAFT] Customer Onboarding OKR

Objective: Help new customers reach first value faster and with less support.

KR1: Increase the share of new accounts that complete the first-value workflow within 7 days from 42% to 60% by the end of Q3.

KR2: Reduce median time from signup to first successful workflow from 3.8 days to under 2 days by the end of Q3.

KR3: Reduce onboarding-related support tickets per new account by 25% from the baseline frozen in the first full week of Q3.

Grade: B+
This is a strong draft because the Objective names a clear customer outcome and each Key Result has an embedded metric, direction, and time frame. The main weakness is that KR3 depends on a frozen baseline and a shared definition of onboarding-related tickets.

Revisions:
1. Define first-value workflow before publishing so KR1 and KR2 cannot drift during the cycle.
2. Freeze the KR3 baseline in the first full week of Q3 and document which ticket categories count.

Open questions:
1. Is the 60% activation target committed or aspirational?
2. Do onboarding tickets include only human-support tickets, or also chatbot and help-center escalations?
