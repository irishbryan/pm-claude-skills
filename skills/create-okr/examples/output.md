# Customer Onboarding OKR

**tl;dr:** Improve onboarding by raising 7-day first-value completion from 42% to 60% while reducing time to value and support demand by the end of Q3.

**Objective:** Help new customers reach first value faster and with less support.

1. KR1: Increase the share of new accounts that complete the first-value workflow within 7 days from 42% to 60% by the end of Q3.

2. KR2: Reduce median time from signup to first successful workflow from 3.8 days to under 2 days by the end of Q3.

3. KR3: Reduce onboarding-related support tickets per new account from 0.42 to 0.30 by the end of Q3.
